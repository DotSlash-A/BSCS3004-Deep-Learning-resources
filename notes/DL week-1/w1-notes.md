# 1.3 Faster-Higher-Stronger

**1. Progress in Deep Learning:**

- The video discusses the advancements in deep learning from the 1950s to the present day.
- It highlights the initial enthusiasm, followed by a period of disappointment due to limitations in training large networks.
- The year 2006 marked a turning point with the development of better optimization methods, activation functions, and regularization techniques.
- This led to faster and more stable training of deep neural networks, enabling them to win prestigious competitions like ImageNet.

**2. Image Processing and Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs):**

- CNNs are particularly successful in image processing tasks.
- The video mentions the challenges of training CNNs with large datasets and limited computational resources in the past.
- Advancements in optimization algorithms, activation functions, and regularization techniques addressed these challenges and improved the performance of CNNs.

**3. Sequence Processing and Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs):**

- RNNs are well-suited for handling sequences like speech and videos.
- The video explains the vanishing and exploding gradient problem that hindered the training of RNNs in the early days.
- The development of Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) cells in 1997 and the availability of powerful GPUs and large datasets in 2014 revitalized the use of RNNs.
- RNNs, particularly LSTMs, dominated the field of NLP and speech processing until the emergence of transformers in 2017.

**4. Game Playing and Deep Reinforcement Learning (RL):**

- Deep RL has achieved remarkable success in complex games like Atari games, Go, and poker.
- The video highlights the breakthroughs in 2015 with RL agents mastering Atari games and defeating human Go champions.
- OpenAI Gym and Gym Retro were released to facilitate research and development in deep RL.
- The video mentions the development of MuZero, a single RL agent capable of playing multiple games like Go, chess, shogi, and Atari, marking a step towards general intelligence.

**Overall, the video emphasizes the significant progress made in deep learning over the past few decades, particularly in image processing, sequence processing, and game playing. It highlights the key challenges and breakthroughs that have shaped the field and points towards the future directions of research in deep learning.**
